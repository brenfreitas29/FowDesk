Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

1. How We Use Cookies

AutoArchitect uses essential cookies and browser storage mechanisms to maintain your secure login session, preserve your active language preferences (English/Spanish), and save custom workflow state.

2. Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential Session Tokens: Secure JWT tokens to authenticate your workspace portal session.
  • Preference Storage: Local storage items for language choice (`autoarch_lang`) and active sector configuration.

3. Managing Cookie Settings

You can adjust your browser settings to decline non-essential cookies. Disabling essential session cookies will require re-authentication upon each visit.
