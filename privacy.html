Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AutoArchitect collects minimal operational metrics necessary to simulate, model, and execute your business workflows safely. This includes user account email addresses, configured workflow node parameters, and simulation execution logs.

2. Data Protection & Security

All payload data, API keys, and workflow configurations are protected in transit using TLS 1.3 encryption and stored securely. We enforce strict row-level security policies (RLS) across all user workspaces.

3. No Data Selling

AutoArchitect does not sell, rent, or monetize customer data or workflow blueprints to third parties. Data is strictly utilized to render your automation platform features.

4. Stripe Payments

Payment processing is handled via hosted Stripe Checkout redirects. AutoArchitect does not store or process raw credit card numbers on our servers.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding your data privacy, please contact our Data Governance Officer at privacy@autoarchitect.com.
